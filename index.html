Atempo Privacy Policy

Atempo is a digital wellbeing application developed by Lumenfold.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Atempo processes information when you use the application.

Atempo is designed to help users become more aware of the time they spend using selected applications by providing usage timers, session tracking, reminders, and usage statistics.

2. App Usage Information

Atempo may use Android's Usage Access functionality to determine when selected applications are being used and to calculate the amount of time spent using them.

The information processed by Atempo may include:

  • Application package names.
  • Application usage duration.
  • Session start and end times.
  • Usage statistics generated from those sessions.

This information is used to provide Atempo's core digital wellbeing features.

3. Local Data Storage

Usage session information generated by Atempo is primarily stored locally on your device.

This data is used to display information such as current session duration, daily usage, and historical usage statistics.

You can remove locally stored Atempo data by clearing the application's storage from Android settings or by uninstalling Atempo.

4. Display Over Other Apps

Atempo may request permission to display content over other applications.

This permission is used to display an optional floating timer while you use applications selected for monitoring.

Atempo does not use this permission to read, modify, or interact with the private content displayed inside other applications.

5. Notifications and Background Monitoring

Atempo may use notifications and Android foreground service functionality to keep usage monitoring active while the application is not visible on screen.

These mechanisms are used only to provide the application's monitoring and awareness features.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Atempo may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand application usage, improve reliability, and diagnose technical problems.

Depending on the Firebase services enabled in the application, technical information may be processed, such as:

  • Device and operating system information.
  • Application version.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • General application interaction and usage events.

Information processed by Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and data processing practices.

7. Data Sharing

Atempo does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase when those services are enabled and necessary to provide analytics, diagnostics, or application infrastructure.

8. Permissions and Special Access

Depending on the features you enable, Atempo may request Android permissions or special access including:

  • Usage Access.
  • Display over other apps.
  • Notification permission.
  • Foreground service functionality.

These permissions are requested only when required for Atempo's functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Atempo is not specifically designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Atempo introduces new functionality, changes how information is processed, or when required to comply with applicable policies or regulations.

Any updates will be published on this page.
